Third-party libraries (self-hosted, byte-identical to upstream)
================================================================

These files are used by year-slideshow.php only (the Year in Review
location photo grid). Do not edit them. To update, download the new
upstream files, verify their hashes and replace them whole.

Verify a file:
    openssl dgst -sha512 -binary <file> | openssl base64 -A
